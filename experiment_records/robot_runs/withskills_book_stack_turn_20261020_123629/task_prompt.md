# Two-book push and full-turn trial

User instruction: Use both grippers to push the two stacked books from one black rectangle fully into the other, rotating the stack one full turn along the way. Keep the books stacked on the table without grasping or lifting them. During execution, retain three video streams and Markdown records.

This is a new trial. Prior experiments and Basic Skill are guidance only.
Use the grippers' external surfaces, with no closing around books or insertion
between the books. Both arms must contribute pushes; move one arm at a time.
Verify table support and relative book alignment after each contact segment.
Track intermediate book orientations to verify a cumulative full turn; identical
start/end orientation alone is insufficient. Verify both complete book footprints
inside the empty destination rectangle after withdrawal and a second stable observation.
Record all outcomes, including partial progress or failure, without substituting
translation alone for the complete requested task.
